arrow_back Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Joheliv Labs handles your information in the Call Recorder application.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All recordings are stored locally on your device.

3. Microphone Access

The app requires microphone access to record audio. Permission is only active when you start recording.

4. Data Storage

All audio files are saved directly to your device localStorage. You have full control and can delete them at any time.
