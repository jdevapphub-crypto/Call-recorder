arrow_back Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Joheliv Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information in the Call Recorder application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience.

2. Information We Collect

Our Call Recorder app is designed with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, contact lists, or audio recordings to external servers. All recordings are stored locally on your device's internal storage.

3. Microphone Access

The app requires microphone access to perform its core function: recording audio. This permission is only active when you explicitly start a recording. We do not listen to or record background audio without your knowledge.

4. Data Storage & Security

All audio files recorded through this app are saved directly to your device. You have full control over these files and can delete them at any time through the app interface or your device's file manager. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the app's local database.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with any third-party analytics or advertising services that track your behavior. Your usage of the app remains completely private.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at Joheliv Labs.
